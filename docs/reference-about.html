About Detail Clinic

Detail Clinic is a trusted automotive detailing shop located at 1201 La Crosse Street, La Crosse, Wisconsin, proudly serving the community for over four years. We specialize in premium interior and exterior detailing, paint protection film (PPF), ceramic paint protection services, and window tinting—designed to keep your vehicle looking its best and protected for the long haul. What began as a mobile detailing service has grown into a dedicated shop built on passion, precision, and care.

We understand that your vehicle is more than just transportation—it’s an investment, a source of pride, and part of your daily life. That’s why every vehicle at Detail Clinic is treated as if it were our own. Using proven techniques, high-quality products, and meticulous attention to detail, we focus on delivering results you can see and service you can feel. When you trust Detail Clinic with your vehicle, you’re choosing experience, honesty, and a team that truly cares.
